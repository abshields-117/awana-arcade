✋ Raised hand = STOP, freeze and look at the leader. Light shoulder tags only; no grabbing, pushing or body contact beyond a gentle tag. Walking / rolling and non-tag roles welcome. Anyone can rest or opt out.
Leader setup & safety checklist

Inspect the space, remove hazards, mark boundaries and keep exits clear. Set an accessible pace and offer a buddy, scorekeeper or signal-helper role. Adults supervise all zones; never leave a child alone. Explain the stop signal and practice it before starting. Stop immediately for a fall or discomfort. No whistles, sound or speech are required.
